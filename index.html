(06) FAQ

Questions studios ask.

What's the difference between motion graphics and motion branding?+

Motion graphics is one video. Motion branding is the set of rules that decides how every future video, screen and post moves.

Do you work with agencies, or with brands directly?+

Mostly with branding studios and agencies, as their motion partner. You own the identity and the client; we define how it moves.

How long does a project take?+

A sprint takes two weeks. A full motion identity system usually takes four to eight weeks, depending on scope.

What do we receive at the end?+

Final films, logo states, a transition kit, editable templates and a written motion guideline your team can follow.

How do revisions work?+

Each stage ends with a written sign-off, so changes happen while they are still cheap, not during final animation.

Which languages do you work in?+

English and Arabic, including Arabic type in motion.
